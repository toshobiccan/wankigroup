CARDSLAYER ROOM ART STYLE — Approved Spawn / Plains reference standard:
PRIMARY VISUAL REFERENCE: assets/art-reference/room-style-approved.png, the user-approved Spawn cottage background. assets/world-background.png is the original secondary Plains reference. Attach and inspect this image for EVERY room generation. Match its actual detail density, rendering, palette relationships and depth. It takes priority over older room instructions and character-derived environment rules.
STYLE: explicit AQW-inspired 2005–2010 Flash fantasy browser-game environment, with original scenery. Angular hand-drawn silhouettes, chunky faceted rocks, geometric leaf clusters, pointed grass shapes and simple architectural silhouettes. A finished gameplay background with the SAME medium level of environmental detail as Plains: neither a bare flat lawn nor richly textured concept art.
LINEWORK: selective dark contours on nearer trees, rocks and terrain edges, thinner internal facet lines, and mostly unoutlined distant silhouettes. Match the Plains image visually; do not apply character-head outline thickness to every environmental object.
COLOR AND SHADING: muted olive grass, slate rock planes and blue atmospheric distances, adapted coherently to each biome. Broad angular cel-shaded planes with restrained tonal variation. Gentle sky/atmospheric transitions are permitted where the Plains reference uses them. No mottled painted grass, realistic material textures, tiny leaf or roof-tile repetition, bloom or glossy 3D lighting.
DEPTH AND COMPOSITION: use the same side-view adventure perspective and layered silhouettes as Plains. Foreground has stronger contrast; mountains, trees and architecture in the distance have lower contrast. Preserve its readable shapes and visual interest without copying its exact cliffs, lake, castle or narrow platform into every room.
WALKABILITY IS SEPARATE FROM STYLE: all unobstructed playable ground is intended to be walkable, not just a painted path. No mandatory yellow strip or narrow lane. Sky, distant scenery, water, cliffs and solid obstacle footprints are not walkable. Use the room-specific ground and collision guide for geometry; never infer collision from a color. Do not bake arrows, markers or collision overlays into artwork.
REFERENCE EXCLUSIONS: only the rejected Spawn v1/v2 previews are excluded; the approved v3 cottage is the primary reference. human-base-v5-front-grip.png is a character reference, not the authority for room rendering. Later rooms also reference the accepted first room of their region for palette consistency, while the approved Spawn cottage remains the style benchmark.
OUTPUT: one coherent room background; no characters, mobs, text, UI, logos or collage. Match Plains, not a painterly fantasy illustration, modern mobile-game scene, isometric map or oversimplified cartoon.
