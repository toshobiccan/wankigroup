Create a 2D fantasy game environment background for CARDSLAYER.

ENVIRONMENT:
[ENVIRONMENT DESCRIPTION]

CARDSLAYER ENVIRONMENT ART STYLE:

Early-to-mid 2000s browser Flash fantasy RPG environment.

The environment must visually belong to the same game as simple thick-outlined cel-shaded fantasy characters.

Use clean, simplified vector-like shapes, bold silhouettes and restrained cel shading.

BACKGROUND DETAIL LEVEL:
LOW.

This is a GAMEPLAY BACKGROUND, not concept art.

The player and enemies will be placed on top of this environment and must remain much more visually prominent than the scenery.

Use large simple shapes rather than many small objects.

LINEWORK:
Dark, clearly visible outlines around major foreground and midground objects.
Thicker outlines on objects closer to the player.
Slightly thinner or softer outlines on distant scenery.
Avoid outlining every tiny environmental feature.

SHADING:
Simple flat colors.
Maximum approximately 2–3 tones per major object.
Large cel-shaded shadow shapes.
No gradients.
No realistic lighting.
No detailed textures.
No photorealistic grass, rocks, water or clouds.

GROUND / WALKABILITY:
VERY IMPORTANT:

The playable walking area must be immediately obvious.

Create a broad, mostly flat foreground and middle-ground surface where characters can stand and move.

Keep the playable ground relatively empty.

Do not cover the walking area with excessive grass, rocks, flowers, bushes or props.

Use environmental boundaries such as water, cliffs, trees, rocks, fences or elevation changes to make non-walkable areas visually obvious.

The environment should communicate:
"This area is playable."
"This area is scenery."

COMPOSITION:
Design the scene so the important gameplay area remains useful when cropped to BOTH:
16:9 landscape
and
9:16 portrait.

Keep essential gameplay terrain and landmarks near the central portion of the composition.

Do not place essential features only at the far left or right edges.

Create enough additional scenery around the central gameplay area to allow responsive cropping.

DEPTH:
Use approximately three simple layers:

1. Foreground / playable ground
2. Midground landmarks
3. Simplified distant background

Do not create excessive atmospheric depth.

COLOR:
Moderately saturated fantasy palette.
Background colors should generally be slightly less intense than character colors.
Strong separation between ground, obstacles and distant scenery.

IMPORTANT STYLE EXCLUSIONS:
NOT realistic.
NOT painterly.
NOT anime landscape.
NOT highly detailed.
NOT 3D.
NOT isometric.
NOT modern mobile-game background.
NOT cinematic concept art.
NOT photorealistic.
NOT soft digital painting.
NOT excessive vegetation.
NOT detailed texture work.
NOT polished AI fantasy artwork.

The result should feel like a hand-drawn vector environment from a fantasy browser MMORPG made around 2005–2010, but with an original visual identity.

No characters.
No enemies.
No UI.
No text.
No logo.
