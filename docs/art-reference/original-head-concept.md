# Original bald head concept — 2026-09-19

Goal: retain expressive 2000s Flash fantasy illustration principles while designing a distinct character. Generate one coherent head before dividing into customizable parts. This pass uses a written brief, without feeding the AQW reproduction back into generation.

Design choices:
- Adult oval skull, broader squared jaw, short flat chin, defined cheek transition.
- Calm, clever expression; narrower almond eyes, modest near/far size difference.
- Moss/amber gradient irises, small pupils, restrained single catchlights.
- Separate mostly level brows with subtle outer arches, distinct from the eyelids.
- Small connected nose bridge and underside shadow; medium-width asymmetric closed smile.
- Warm olive-tan complexion, anatomically attached small ears.
- Dark-brown confident contour; upper-left light with a continuous far-side cheek/jaw shadow and limited deeper accents.
- Transparent background, bald head only. No clothing, hair, decorative markings or texture.

Review the assembled proportions first. No gameplay replacement or modular decomposition in this concept pass. Original-design intent is not a legal clearance or guarantee.

Selected concept for review: dev/reference/original-bald-head-v2.png. Revision 2 simplifies the overly detailed first pass: solid brows, minimal nose/mouth marks, three flat skin tones, stronger outlines. Both studies retained for comparison.

## Revision 3: cartoon wardrobe master

User direction: second design is preferred but needs less detail, larger eyes and compatibility with wardrobe customization.

Generate one assembled master using v2 as reference. Enlarge eyes about 30% horizontally / 45% vertically; keep adult jaw proportions. Simplify nose to a small stroke, mouth to one closed smile line, ears to one inner fold. Remove sculpted bridge, lip shading and scattered facial patches. Keep broad continuous skin shading; gradient irises remain.

Future extraction must retain the master canvas coordinates: head/skin base, overlapping ears, independent eye whites/irises, matching-color eyebrows and mouth. Do not independently scale auto-trimmed features to guessed rectangles. Blinks must use the same eye registration. Hair covers the completed skull, with continuous artwork behind overlaid ears and no ear holes. Do not integrate or split until the assembled design is accepted.
